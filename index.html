About me

I am a third-year PhD student at the Artificial Intelligence for Drug Discovery (AIDD) Laboratory, Zhejiang University, supervised by Prof. Tingjun Hou. My work resides at the intersection of machine learning and quantum chemistry.

My research focuses on Deep Potential frameworks, specifically the Quantum Chemical Information Injection (QCII) mechanism. I aim to bridge microscopic electronic states with macroscopic pharmacological properties, empowering the entire drug discovery pipeline—from 3D molecular generation to binding free energy prediction.

Featured Frameworks

LiTENexus Ecosystem

An end-to-end virtual drug discovery system built on the LiTEN-Base operator. It provides quantum-accurate molecular representations to empower generation, screening, and profiling tasks.

LaMGen

An LLM-based 3D molecular generation framework that aligns latent language space with physical 3D coordinates for multi-target drug design.
