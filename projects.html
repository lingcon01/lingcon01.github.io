LiTENexus: End-to-End Virtual Drug Discovery System

LiTENexus is an end-to-end virtual drug discovery ecosystem based on quantum chemical grounding. Driven by a novel Quantum Chemical Information Injection (QCII) mechanism, the foundational LiTEN-Base neural operator acts as the central engine.

By explicitly integrating quantum evolution laws, LiTENexus establishes a robust mathematical bridge between the microscopic quantum realm and macroscopic pharmacological metrics. It operates as a continuous closed-loop workflow that encompasses molecular generation (LaMGen), conformation optimization (LiTEN-FF), ultra-fast virtual screening (LiTENCLIP), ADMET profiling (LiTEN-ADMET), and multi-objective optimization (Mol-Craft), completely redefining the efficiency and physical interpretability of automated drug design.

LaMGen: LLM-Based 3D Molecular Generation

LaMGen leverages Large Language Models to conduct 3D molecular generation specifically for multi-target drug design tasks. By integrating linguistic representations of chemical spaces with precise 3D spatial coordinate decoding, LaMGen enables rational generation of hit compounds that simultaneously fulfill complex pharmacophore and affinity constraints.
